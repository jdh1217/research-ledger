A comparison note positions your work against the closest existing approaches.
It is the raw material for a Related Work section and for the moment a reviewer
asks what exactly is new here.

Two failure modes to avoid:

  Overclaiming. Every dimension where you differ is not automatically a
  dimension where you are better. Some differences are trade-offs; say so.

  Strawmanning. Compare against what each approach actually does, quoting the
  source. If you cannot state a competing approach in terms its authors would
  accept, you do not understand it well enough to differentiate from it.
